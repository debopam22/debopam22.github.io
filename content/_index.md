<span style="font-size: 0.9em;">I am a fifth-year Ph.D. candidate <a href="https://mitchell.tamu.edu">Mitchell Institute for Fundamental Physics and Astronomy</a>, <a href="https://www.tamu.edu/index.html">Texas A&M University</a>, College Station, TX, USA, working with Prof. Bhaskar Dutta, and I expect to complete my Ph.D. in August 2027. My research sits at the intersection of particle and astroparticle phenomenology and machine learning, spanning both laboratory and astrophysical searches for sub-GeV dark matter (DM). In lab-based scenarios, I have investigated heavy neutral lepton production via beam-dump neutrino up-scattering and identified kinematic signatures to enhance signal-to-background discrimination. I have also analyzed monophoton final-state signatures from 2-to-3 DM-nucleus scattering at the Deep Underground Neutrino Experiment (DUNE) near detector (ND) and the Short-Baseline Neutrino program. Additionally, I used blip signatures and incorporated state-of-the-art ab initio nuclear calculations to enhance the Short-Baseline Near Detector (SBND) sensitivity. Astrophysically, I proposed cosmic-ray sub-GeV DM scattering as a potential explanation for the Galactic Center gamma-ray excess and also derived improved constraints on light-scalar mediator models from 2-to-3 and 2-to-4 scattering of cosmic and blazar-boosted DM across various experiments. My ongoing work includes di-tau signatures at the DUNE ND, simulation-based inference using XGBoost and multi-layer perceptron (MLP) classifiers for source discrimination and kinematic parameter inference at SBND, and a simultaneous explanation of the Galactic Center GeV and the 20 GeV gamma-ray excesses. While I remain deeply interested in continuing these searches for sub-GeV masses, I am also motivated to search a much broader range of possible DM masses using the diverse phenomenological tools I have developed. More broadly, I am interested in leveraging artificial intelligence to automate modeling, simulation, and scientific discovery in particle and astroparticle physics and related disciplines.</span>
